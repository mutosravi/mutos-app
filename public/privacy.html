Privacy

This app is used only by Muto's Studio (Lucknow) and the photographers, videographers and assistants who work with the studio, to see and plan wedding shoot schedules.

What we use from your Google account

Only your name and email address, to check that you are on the studio's team list and show you the right page. We do not read your Gmail, contacts, Drive or anything else in your Google account.

What the app stores

Shoot schedules prepared by the studio: dates, venues, roles, equipment, team names and phone numbers, and the client contact for each shoot. Crew members see only their own shoots. The data is kept in a secured database and is not sold or shared with anyone outside the studio.

Removing your access

Ask the studio to remove your email from the team list. Your login stops working immediately and your schedule page is deleted.

Contact

Muto's Studio, Lucknow. Message the studio on WhatsApp, or reply to the person who invited you.
